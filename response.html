About 3YESES

Every talent deserves a stage.

3YESES started with a simple idea: talented people everywhere should have the chance to be seen. Not just the ones with connections or big-city access — everyone. From diverse communities around the world, we're building a place where creatives can showcase their work, build a name for themselves, and get discovered on their own terms.

Why we built this

The creative industry has always had a visibility problem. Talented actors, musicians, models, dancers, voice artists, photographers, and performers of all kinds exist in every corner of the world — but most never get the chance to be seen beyond their local community.

We wanted to change that. 3YESES was born from the belief that talent doesn't have a postcode. Whether you're a street dancer in Accra, a classical violinist in Mumbai, a voice artist in Manchester, or a model in Buenos Aires — you deserve a professional platform to showcase your work and get noticed.

We built 3YESES to be radically simple and affordable. No gatekeeping, no approval process to join, no per-booking fees. One subscription gives you everything: unlimited uploads, a full professional profile, analytics to track your growth, and priority search placement so you actually get discovered.

This is your stage. Make it count.

What we stand for

The principles that guide everything we build.

Open to Everyone

No gatekeeping. No auditions to join. If you have talent, you belong here — regardless of where you come from, your background, or how much experience you have.

Diversity First

We celebrate every genre, style, and culture. From classical musicians in Tokyo to street dancers in Lagos to voice artists in São Paulo — every creative deserves a stage.

Community Driven

Browse, like, comment, and share. Our hub brings performances and curated media together so creatives can inspire each other and build real connections.

Fair & Affordable

One simple subscription — £10 for 6 months or £20 for 12 months. No commissions, no hidden fees, no premium tiers. Everyone gets the same full access.
